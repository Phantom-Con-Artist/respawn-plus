# Website: landing page (and the site around it)

Mode: Persuade. Scope: website/ (Astro), the landing page first, with Docs, Downloads and About in the same world.

Audience: mod-curious Linux players. Job: understand that Vessel runs a Windows game together with its mod manager, script extender and trainer as one room, and follow releases. Proof: the real launcher UI (rendered mock library with popular titles, labelled as a showcase) and the verified Skyrim + MO2 + SKSE + trainer run. Constraint: say plainly it is a showcase build, tested with Skyrim Special Edition; claim nothing else. Cover art: public Steam header images, illustration only.

Mood brief from the user: nostalgic, lonesome yet attractive, surreal futuristic; neon night cities seen from a quiet vantage, a vast glowing horizon, one viewer. Evoke the feeling, never redraw the reference scenes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Vessel is the carriage of the last train home. The visitor sits alone at the window while the night city slides past; each station is one part of the product. Refuses the split hero with a screenshot beside a headline and a feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Carriage interior near-black #07090f with warm sodium light #ff7a3d at the edges; the window is the page's main surface, deep blue night #0e1d2b over a seeded canvas city of lit windows; the horizon line is the logo's cyan #18e6e4, the only cyan besides the primary action. Big Shoulders Display, condensed transit signage caps, for headlines and station signs; Hanken Grotesk for text. Square corners, window mullions and carriage rails as the dividers; no cards, no glass, no gradients standing in for material.

STORY: The visitor boards (first viewport), passes stations (find your games, one room per game, the companions board together, one press, the honest showcase note), sees the launcher library lit like a departure board, and at the terminus follows releases.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Carriage wall with the wordmark and nav; a huge two-line condensed headline top left; the next-station sign at right; a full-width window band (about half the height) with the city sliding past and the cyan horizon; under it the one-line lead, the cyan Follow releases button and the Build from source link; a small amber line saying showcase, tested with Skyrim.

FORM: The Night Train, 5th on the ordered list. seed:3c8e5c62e236389f. Raises: departure-board discipline for the library (fixed columns, row per game, state restyles the row); the city generated from a seeded sequence on canvas; outside light follows the visitor's local hour; scroll is one crank driving every layer.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
